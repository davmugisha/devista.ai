## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners, founders and operators of growing companies (inferred: roughly 10 to 200
people) who are weighing whether to hand operational work and software delivery to AI. They are
not engineers. They read the page to decide one thing: whether Devista can take real work off
their team, and ship their software, without putting the business at risk.

## Product Purpose

Devista AI (devista.ai, a David LLC company) deploys an autonomous AI OS and a parallel
software fleet for a business: 24/7 multi-agent departments, custom engineering and automated
revenue pipelines. Success for a visitor is booking the free 15-Minute Process Audit Call.

## Positioning

Devista does not sell a tool to configure. It staffs ten business functions with AI
departments, each with a lead agent and a skill tree, keeps people in charge of the decisions
that carry risk, and caps what every department can spend. Work is split into three execution
tiers: Human-Led (10%), AI-Assisted (30%) and 24/7 Autonomous (60%).

## Operating Context

The engagement runs in four steps: Start (process audit), Build (AI OS and Orca fleets), Sell
(AI Sales OS), Scale (Paperclip budget caps). Clients keep their existing tools (CRM,
accounting, support desk, chat).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Ten departments: Software Dev, Engineering, Design, Marketing, Sales, Deals, Operations,
  Finance, Legal, Support.
- Systems shown on the page: Paperclip (budget caps and approvals), Orca ADE (parallel
  software fleet), Inspo MCP (design and brand work), Claude Code CLI (agents doing the work),
  plus connected tools such as HubSpot, QuickBooks, Stripe, GitHub and Slack.
- Guardrails: a hard budget cap per department, approval before risky actions, a complete
  activity record.
- Offers: One-Time Implementation Builds ($5,000 to $18,000) and Fractional AI & Dev Retainers
  ($2,500 to $8,000 per month). Scope and final price are set after the free audit call.
- Booking: a LeadConnector calendar. The link in the brief is the reference site's own
  calendar and must be replaced with Devista's before launch.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and wordmark: DEVISTA.AI with a deep cyan badge mark (#086B91).
- Bright canvas (#F6FBFE), white surfaces (#FFFFFF), pale cyan panels (#EAF5FA) and fine blue-grey borders (#D5E5EC).
- Dark navy for primary actions, deep cyan for active states and amber for waiting or escalated states.
- Voice: plain business English, sentence case; selling ahead of engineering.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet: no client logos, testimonials, case studies, certifications or measured results.
Nothing of that kind may be invented. The console is labelled "Sample run" and every other
illustrative figure is labelled "Example".

## Product Principles

1. Sell the outcome in the owner's language, with the system that delivers it named beside it.
2. Show control before capability: every department has a cap, an approver and a record.
3. Be transparent: price ranges, the process and the first call are stated up front.
4. One live proof (the console) beats a page of claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, full keyboard use, a pause control on the live console, reduced-motion
support, layouts from 360px to 1440px.
