## Marketing site rules

This repo is the marketing site for Saydeed (saydeed.com), Next.js 16 App Router and Tailwind 4. Saydeed is the only brand: use `SITE` from `lib/site.ts` and never write the name by hand. The site's one job is booked calls that turn into a paid Agent Team Setup. The product behind it is Saydeed Work (app.saydeed.com, a different repo). `redesign/HANDOFF.md` explains how the site got here. Earlier builds are archived in `.unlazy/retired-<date>/`.

### Tokens

- All colours, type sizes, radii, shadows, widths and easing live in the `@theme` block of `app/globals.css`. The Tailwind defaults are cleared (`--*: initial`), so only token utilities exist: `text-ink`, `bg-mist`, `rounded-frame`, `max-w-measure`. Spacing is any multiple of 4px (`--spacing: 0.25rem`).
- The site is light with a soft cyan hero. Colour names are roles: `paper` is the page, `mist` a white raised surface, `panel` a pale cyan surface inside a surface, `rule` a hairline, `rule-strong` a form-control border, `ink` / `graphite` / `slate` the three text levels.
- Dark is the default (David, 2026-10-13): `app/layout.tsx` serves `<html data-theme="dark">`. Light is chosen with the switch in the footer (`ThemeToggle`), saved in this browser, and restored before first paint by the inline script in `app/layout.tsx`. The dark values live in `:root[data-theme="dark"]` in `app/globals.css`: the same roles, darker values. So use role utilities only, never `text-white`, `bg-black` or a colour that assumes a light page; `text-paper` on `bg-ink` inverts correctly. `deep` / `deep-2` are a surface that stays dark in both themes (the featured offer), with `text-on-signal` and the `inverse` button on it. Shadows mix from `shade`, never `ink`. Accent text is `signal-bright`, never `signal`, which is a fill colour.
- One accent, `signal` (deep cyan), for focus, selected and approved states, always with `text-on-signal` where it fills a control. Primary calls to action use dark `ink`. Use `signal-bright` for accent text and icons on light. `glow`, `glow-cyan` and `glow-deep` are light sources for decoration only, never text. `alert` is for errors and `hold` for waiting or escalated states.
- No hex, `rgb()`, or arbitrary `[12px]` values outside `app/globals.css` and `lib/tokens.ts`. `lib/tokens.ts` mirrors the colours for places CSS can't reach (Open Graph images in `lib/og.tsx`, `themeColor`). Change both together. The one exception is tool logos in `ToolMarquee`, which keep their brand colour from `simple-icons`.
- Type: Instrument Sans only (through `next/font`), normal width. Headings use `font-display` and `tracking-display`. Body copy stays within `max-w-measure` (68ch).
- Radii by role: `rounded-chip` for tags, `rounded-control` for inputs, `rounded-window` for product windows, `rounded-frame` for cards, `rounded-card` for the hero window, and `rounded-dot` for CTA pills, dots and small badges.
- Effects are classes in `app/globals.css`, not one-off utilities: `hero-sky` (white and cyan hero wash), `hero-showcase` (stronger cyan behind the product), `glow-pool`, `card` / `card-lit` / `card-quiet`, `orb`, `sweep`, `flow`, `tilt` and `parallax-near` / `parallax-far` (driven by `HeroStage`).
- Motion: scroll reveals through `data-reveal` (with `delay(ms)` from `components/Section.tsx` to stagger), armed by the inline script in `app/layout.tsx` and run by `RevealObserver`. Content must stay visible without JavaScript. Hero and page intros use `animate-hero` on load. Loops (`animate-float`, `animate-marquee`, typing, auto-cycling diagrams) are decorative. One `prefers-reduced-motion` rule in `globals.css` stops every animation; client components that animate in JavaScript check it themselves.

### Components

- `ButtonLink` / `buttonClass` (`components/Button.tsx`): variants are `primary`, `secondary`, `quiet` and `inverse` (white, only on the `deep` surface). Pass `track="<location>"` on every call to action. Only primary links prefetch.
- Section layout: `Section` (chip, centred h2, one-sentence lede, content) for one buyer question per section; `SectionHead` and `Chip` when a section needs its own layout; `PageIntro` for each inner page's chip, h1 and call to action under the glow; `CtaBand` for the closing glowing card.
- The home page is service first, modelled on altari.ai (David, 2026-10-11): it sells the problem, the agent team, the steps, what the buyer gets and who they work with. It shows no app screens. In order: `Hero` (problem, offer, agent team grid), `ToolMarquee`, `Pains`, how it works (`StageCards`, each step ending in "You get"), `CostCalculator`, `AgentTeam` (org chart, the buyer picks where to start), `Deliverables`, `Pricing`, `Founder`, FAQ, `CtaBand`.
- The hero message is David's pitch in short (2026-10-11): the agency leaks hours between tools and manual work; we fix it in 7 days; the team and AI agents become one system, with agents doing the repeat work in every department around the clock and people approving what goes out. `SITE.tagline` and `SITE.description` carry the same message. The FAQ is altari.ai's pattern: one centred column of separate rounded cards (`Faq`), closing with "Still got questions?" and the booking button. `Pricing` follows the ui-layouts pricing-overview block from 21st.dev: the Agent Team Setup as a wide `deep` card with the founding badge, the other two offers as framed cards, each with "Book a free call" and a calendar icon and both lists (what you get, what you decide), then the four steps after booking. The cost calculator is one compact two-column card.
- App renderings live only on `/product`, in `components/screens/`, with the `Window` frame (`role="img"` with a full `aria-label`, example data, a caption saying so). Interactive examples (`ApprovalsDemo`, `HubDiagram`, `AgentTeam`, `CostCalculator`, `Pricing` toggle, the workflow explorer on `/how-it-works`) use real buttons and say they are examples. `MediaFrame` holds real screenshots and video and shows a labelled placeholder until `src` is set.
- Where a template would show client logos, quotes or case-study numbers, use the honest replacements: `ToolMarquee` (tools agencies use, shown with David's approval while he builds the connections, with the note that which of the visitor's tools are connected first is agreed on the call), `Founder` (David by name, his facts and the `COMMITMENTS`), example agents and workflows, and a `PORTFOLIO` list in `lib/content.ts` that stays empty until a past client has agreed in writing. `Pricing` shows the three real offers.
- `FOUNDER` in `lib/site.ts` holds David's name, role and facts. His photo goes in `public/founder/` with `FOUNDER.photo` set to its path; until then a labelled placeholder shows. The booking card names him as host.
- Forms: `Field` and `controlClass` for inputs. Every input needs a label and an error linked with `aria-describedby`. `Honeypot` and `CheckBadge` live there too. Validation rules live in `lib/booking-validate.ts`, `lib/newsletter.ts` and `lib/email.ts`, and run on both the client and the server.
- Shared copy (offers, prices, steps, pains, agent team, commitments, FAQ, controls) lives in `lib/content.ts`, and the example workflows in `lib/workflows.ts`. Each fact is stated once there.
- The workflow interactive renders its first state on the server (`ExplorerView`) and takes over on the client (`ExplorerIsland` → `WorkflowExplorer`). Keep the markup shared.
- Analytics go only through `track()` in `lib/analytics.ts` (`cta_click`, `form_start`, `form_submit`, `form_error`).

### Copy

- The primary action is always `CTA.book` in `lib/site.ts` ("Book a free call", linking to `/book#calendar`), with the same words everywhere. The secondary action is `CTA.how` ("See how it works") in the home hero and `CTA.product` ("See the product") elsewhere. The path is: free 30 minute call, then a written scope and an invoice, then the work starts only after payment.
- The buyer is a small agency first (founders and solo operators second). Write to the repeat work they already do by hand: lead research, outreach drafts, client reports, proposals, status updates, invoice chasing. Never imply the buyer has no systems.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons. No em dashes, no arrows on labels, no "Get started" or "Learn more".
- Never use: revolutionize, unlock, supercharge, seamless, cutting-edge, next-generation, game-changing, leverage, synergy, empower, "transform your business", or "AI-powered" as a badge. Never pitch headcount cuts or "bigger business without a bigger payroll".
- No invented proof: no customer logos, testimonials, statistics, percentages, certifications or claimed results, and no ROI multiple such as "3X ROI" until a paying client's measured result backs it. Tool names appear only in `ToolMarquee`, never as a claimed partnership. The cost calculator shows the cost of the visitor's own repeat work from their own inputs, never a promised saving. Past projects appear only as portfolio, only with the client's written permission, and never as Saydeed customers. Illustrations carry an "example data" caption. The business is not registered anywhere yet: never write a company type, registration or legal entity.
- Prices are real and shown in US dollars, written `US$1,500`. The only prices are the three offers in `lib/content.ts` (Agent Team Setup, AI Ops Install, custom build). Everything is paid upfront. Do not add any other figure, apart from the revenue ranges the booking form offers as answers (`BOOKING.revenueRanges`).
- Worldwide audience: no US-only date or phone formats, and the visitor's time zone detected and shown by name.

### Booking and sign-ups

- The site's one public address is `CONTACT.email` in `lib/site.ts`. Link it only through `ContactLink` (`components/ContactLink.tsx`), never type it by hand. Every booking also invites it as a guest (`guestList` in `lib/booking-event.ts`).
- `/book` posts to `app/api/book/route.ts`, which creates the event with a Meet link on the host's Google Calendar, and Google emails the invite. It needs `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` and `GOOGLE_REFRESH_TOKEN` (see `.env.example` and `npm run booking:auth`). Hours, notice and copy live in `lib/booking-config.ts`. The form requires first name, work email, agency name, an annual revenue range ("Prefer not to say" allowed) and the one task to hand to agents (at least `TOPIC_MIN` characters). Last name, phone, website and team size are optional. The website field is `site`, because `website` is the honeypot. Before any deploy run `node scripts/verify-site.mjs <banned|offer|brand|dashes|tokens|facts>` and `npm run build`. A push to `main` deploys to saydeed.com: `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` runs those checks, then `deploy/deploy.sh` (setup in `deploy/README.md`).
- The footer sign-up posts to `app/api/subscribe/route.ts`, which appends to the CSV at `SUBSCRIBERS_FILE`.
- Every API route reads its body through `readForm` (`lib/api-http.ts`) and rate-limits with `allow` (`lib/api-guard.ts`).
- A form shows its confirmation only when the calendar event or the saved address exists. Never add a path that confirms without delivering.
