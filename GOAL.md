## Honest verdict

- **US$15k is realistic but not comfortable.** It needs about 7 paid installs, and the plan reaches the first one in week 6. That leaves 6 weeks for the other 6, and the assumed 25 percent close rate has no evidence behind it.
- **US$40k is a stretch.** It only happens if at least one agency buys the AI Ops Install or a custom build. Treat it as a bonus, not a plan.
- **US$100k is not reachable by 31 Dec from a cold start.** Keep it as a direction, not a target.
- The strongest lever is the first paid client. Until then, the site matters less than the payment rail, the demo and the number of real conversations.
- The biggest hidden risk is the first install. A 7 day promise to a stranger, built on a product where only Work is usable, has to be delivered. Pick the first workflow carefully (lead research and outreach drafting is the one David has shown himself).
