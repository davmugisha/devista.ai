The model
You stay in command. The work gets done anyway.

Your operational work splits into three tiers, so leadership time only goes where judgment is needed.

10%
30% AI-assisted
60% runs on its own, 24/7
Human-led · 10%

Strategic decisions and executive approvals: pricing and key deals, hiring and budget, sign-off on anything client-facing.

AI-assisted · 30%

A co-pilot drafts and your team reviews: proposals and contracts, campaigns and content, forecasts and reports.

Autonomous · 60%

Background work that never clocks out: follow-ups and reminders, data entry and CRM, invoicing and ticket triage.
